Core Algorithm

K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) Mapping

At the heart of Credify is a sophisticated KNN architecture. When evaluating a new profile, the AI does not rely on static thresholds. Instead, it places the application within a multi-dimensional spatial cloud of historical data.

  • adjust
    Spatial Plotting Your financial metrics act as coordinates, plotting a unique point in our vast data universe.
  • scatter_plot
    Proximity Analysis The model calculates the Euclidean distance to identify the 'K' most similar historical profiles.
  • insights
    Predictive Consensus Risk is determined by the aggregated performance of these nearest neighbors, ensuring highly contextual accuracy.
